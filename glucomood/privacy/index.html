Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Overview

GlucoMood is a friendly diabetes companion for manual logging, movement motivation, and supportive Mimi AI conversations. This Privacy Policy explains what information GlucoMood uses and how it is handled.

Manual health logs

Logged medication, insulin, and blood glucose entries currently stay on your device. GlucoMood itself does not upload those manual entries to a GlucoMood server.

Apple Health

Apple Health access is optional. GlucoMood only reads supported Apple Health activity data after you grant permission in iOS.

Supported Apple Health data currently includes:

  • Steps
  • Walking + Running Distance
  • Workouts / Exercise Activity
  • Workout / Exercise Duration

GlucoMood uses this activity data for movement progress, Health-related progress displays, and optional Mimi Movement Message rewards. GlucoMood does not write fake activity data to Apple Health.

You can change Apple Health permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

Mimi AI

Messages sent to Mimi AI are sent to a backend service so Mimi can generate a reply. Mimi is designed to provide supportive, conversational guidance and general diabetes education, not medical diagnosis or emergency care.

Do not use Mimi as a replacement for professional medical care. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call emergency services immediately.

Notifications

GlucoMood may use local notifications for supported reminders, such as step-goal notifications. You can manage notification permissions in iOS Settings.

Third-party services and trademarks

GlucoMood may use Apple Health data only with your permission. Mimi AI replies are generated through a backend service connected to an AI provider.
